# Design System: Aqua Flow

## 1. Overview & Creative North Star
The Creative North Star for Freshfold is **"Aqua Flow."**

Laundry is about the transformation from dirty/heavy to clean/light. The UI should reflect this lightness. We move away from bulky, shadowed cards to **Luminous Layers** and **Fluid Transitions**. The aesthetic is inspired by high-end wellness and home automation apps—clean, airy, and effortlessly efficient.

## 2. Colors: The Palette of Purity
We use colors that evoke the feeling of fresh water and crisp linens.

- **Primary (Sky Blue - #00A3FF):** Represents clean water and the sky. Used for primary actions and highlights.
- **Secondary (Mint Fresh - #00D6A3):** Represents freshness and eco-friendly care. Used for success states and promotions.
- **Background (Mist - #F0F9FF):** A very light, airy blue that feels cleaner than pure white.
- **Surface (Pure - #FFFFFF):** Used for content containers.

### The "Frosted Glass" Rule
To add a premium feel, use Backdrop Blurs (`20px - 40px`) on headers and floating navigation. This creates a sense of depth and translucency, like looking through clean water.

## 3. Typography: Clarity & Warmth
- **Headlines (Montserrat):** A geometric sans-serif that feels modern and approachable.
- **Body (Inter):** The standard for legibility, ensuring service details and prices are crystal clear.

## 4. Shapes & Spacing: Organic Rhythm
- **Rounding:** Use generous 24px corners for main cards and 12px for buttons.
- **Spacing:** Avoid cramped layouts. Use an 8dp grid with 24dp margins to allow the UI to "breathe."

## 5. Components & Animations
- **Service Cards:** Instead of flat boxes, use cards with a subtle inner glow and soft, diffused shadows. 
- **Micro-Animations:** Use "Spring" physics for card expansions. Add a subtle "shimmer" effect to active states to suggest cleanliness.
- **Floating Actions:** The "Add to Cart" or "Schedule" buttons should float with a gentle hover animation, making them feel reliable and ready to serve.
